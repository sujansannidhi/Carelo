What Carelo does

Six quiet helpers, one calm picture.

Health Integration Hub

Everything her phone and watch already know, in one place.

Carelo reads vitals and activity from Apple Health, Google Health and compatible smartwatches, then organizes them into one simple card for you.

  • Heart rate, blood pressure, steps and sleep
  • No new devices to buy

Smart medication & daily check-in

One full-screen button. No menus.

You set the schedule. At dose time, Mom sees a single “Took Medicine” button. A missed dose alerts you right away, and Carelo flags patterns like several misses in a row.

About half of medications for chronic conditions aren't taken as prescribed. Viswanathan M, et al. Ann Intern Med. 2012;157(11):785–795.

Weight tracking by phone scan

Her old bathroom scale, now connected.

A small reference sticker goes on the scale she already has. She points her phone at the display, the camera reads the number, and Carelo tracks the trend and flags unusual changes.

Cognitive mini-tests

A two-minute game that builds a long-term picture.

Short memory and word games are scored over time. The reports can help you and her doctor spot early signs of change. Carelo doesn't diagnose anything.

7.4 million Americans 65+ live with Alzheimer's, about 1 in 9. Alzheimer's Association, 2026 Alzheimer's Disease Facts and Figures.

Safety “I'm Okay” check-in

A simple question at the times you choose.

Carelo asks “Are you okay?” at the times you pick. If there's no answer within the window, you're notified, so silence never goes unnoticed.

Wellness Score & weekly report

One number, built from the things that matter.

Medication adherence, weight stability, cognitive results and check-ins roll up into a single score. Every week you get a trend report with anything that needs a closer look.
